# Blue Taxi v1.0.0-beta

Play Store review is still pending. These are the official Android builds until the listing is live.

## Customer app

- **File:** `blue-taxi-v1.0.0-beta.apk`
- **Package:** `com.bluetaxi.booking`
- **Version:** 1.0.0-beta (30)
- **Requires:** Android 7.0 or later

### Changelog

- Book a taxi as a guest without creating an account
- See estimated fare and booking fee as separate amounts before you confirm
- Track the taxi after a driver accepts, including name, plate, taxi number, and brand
- Pay the driver in cash: meter fare plus booking fee
- Rate the driver after the ride, or skip

## Driver app

- **File:** `blue-taxi-driver-v1.0.0-beta.apk`
- **Package:** `com.app.bluetaxi.driver`
- **Version:** 1.0.0-beta (36)
- **Requires:** Android 7.0 or later

### Changelog

- Sign in with the login the office creates. There is no public registration
- Go online and accept taxi bookings
- The booking fee is taken from the driver wallet when you accept
- Meter cash stays with the driver
- Confirm the meter amount at the end of the ride

## Install

1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow installs from this source.
3. Tap **Install**, then open the app.

Install only from this repository.
